# Hexvault — Support

## Contact

For help, questions, or feedback about Hexvault: **rockvillecodymaryland@gmail.com**

Include your watchOS/iOS versions and the feature involved when reporting a problem. Response times vary.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is my Hexvault data stored?**
Entirely on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Hexvault has no servers, accounts, or analytics — nothing you do in the app leaves your devices.

**Why does a feature ask for Health, Location, Microphone, or Motion access?**
Each tool requests only the permission it needs, the first time you use it (for example, Compass and Find My Car use location; Voice Notes uses the microphone). You can change any permission later in Settings or the Health app.

**My watch data isn't appearing on my iPhone.**
Both apps share data through the system App Group. Make sure the iPhone companion app has been opened at least once, then relaunch the watch app. Restarting both devices resolves most sync gaps.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete the item in the app, or delete the app itself — all Hexvault data is stored locally, so removing the app removes everything (subject to your device backups).

# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-17
**Last updated:** 2026-05-17

## Summary in one paragraph

Hexvault runs entirely on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Nothing you do in Hexvault leaves your devices. We do not run servers, do not collect analytics, do not include any third-party SDKs, and do not have user accounts. There is nothing for us to share with anyone because we never receive your data in the first place.

## What Hexvault accesses on your devices

- **HealthKit data** (heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, water, caffeine, mindful minutes). Used only to display readings inside Hexvault and to log water/caffeine/mindful sessions you create. Read and written via Apple’s HealthKit API, which keeps all data on your devices. Hexvault requests these scopes explicitly the first time a feature needs them; you can revoke any of them in the Health app at any time.
- **Location** (when in use). Used by Compass, Find My Car, Trail Breadcrumbs, and Park Timer. Coordinates are stored only in your device’s App Group container so the iPhone companion can show them; they are never transmitted off-device.
- **Microphone**. Used only when you tap to record a Voice Note. Audio is saved as a file on your watch and transferred to your iPhone via the system App Group.
- **Motion & Fitness**. Used by Bubble Level, Protractor, Altimeter, Posture Check, and the watch’s water-submersion sensor (Series 10). Sensor readings are processed in-memory and not stored.
- **Reminders**. Quick Reminders dictated on the watch are saved to the iPhone’s Reminders app via EventKit.
- **Notifications**. The Park Timer schedules a local notification 5 minutes before expiry. No remote/push notifications.

## What Hexvault does NOT do

- We do not transmit any data to any server.
- We do not include analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Amplitude, Firebase, Segment, etc.).
- We do not include advertising SDKs.
- We do not include crash-reporting services beyond Apple’s built-in opt-in crash reports.
- We do not have user accounts, signups, or logins.
- We do not sell, share, or rent your data because we never collect it.

## Third parties
