# Holster — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-17
**Last updated:** 2026-05-17

## Summary in one paragraph

Holster runs entirely on your Apple Watch and iPhone. Nothing you do in Holster leaves your devices. We do not run servers, do not collect analytics, do not include any third-party SDKs, and do not have user accounts. There is nothing for us to share with anyone because we never receive your data in the first place.

## What Holster accesses on your devices

- **HealthKit data** (heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, water, caffeine, mindful minutes). Used only to display readings inside Holster and to log water/caffeine/mindful sessions you create. Read and written via Apple’s HealthKit API, which keeps all data on your devices. Holster requests these scopes explicitly the first time a feature needs them; you can revoke any of them in the Health app at any time.
- **Location** (when in use). Used by Compass, Find My Car, Trail Breadcrumbs, and Park Timer. Coordinates are stored only in your device’s App Group container so the iPhone companion can show them; they are never transmitted off-device.
- **Microphone**. Used only when you tap to record a Voice Note. Audio is saved as a file on your watch and transferred to your iPhone via the system App Group.
- **Motion & Fitness**. Used by Bubble Level, Protractor, Altimeter, Posture Check, and the watch’s water-submersion sensor (Series 10). Sensor readings are processed in-memory and not stored.
- **Reminders**. Quick Reminders dictated on the watch are saved to the iPhone’s Reminders app via EventKit.
- **Notifications**. The Park Timer schedules a local notification 5 minutes before expiry. No remote/push notifications.

## What Holster does NOT do

- We do not transmit any data to any server.
- We do not include analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Amplitude, Firebase, Segment, etc.).
- We do not include advertising SDKs.
- We do not include crash-reporting services beyond Apple’s built-in opt-in crash reports.
- We do not have user accounts, signups, or logins.
- We do not sell, share, or rent your data because we never collect it.

## Third parties

Holster does not integrate with any third party.

## Children

Holster is suitable for general audiences. The App Store age rating is 4+. Holster does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If we ever change this policy in a way that meaningfully expands what Holster accesses, we’ll update the effective date above and note the change in the app’s release notes.
